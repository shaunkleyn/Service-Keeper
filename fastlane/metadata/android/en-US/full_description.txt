Service Keeper monitors background services, accessibility services, and notification listeners killed or disabled by the system, and automatically restarts or re-enables them using Shizuku for privileged shell access (no root required).

Features:

* Monitors background services and restarts them when killed
* Monitors accessibility services and re-enables them if revoked
* Monitors notification listeners and re-enables them if disabled
* Configurable per-service check interval, from 5 minutes to 4+ hours
* Full timestamped audit log of every detected stop, restart attempt, and outcome
* Reschedules all monitors automatically after a device reboot
* Per-app toggle for restart notifications
* App relaunch that restores whatever app you were using afterward, with configurable timing (always, only when idle, only when locked, etc.)

Requirements:

* Android 8.0 (API 26) or newer
* Shizuku, installed and running (paired via Wireless Debugging or root)

Service Keeper does not require or request any network permissions and does not collect or transmit any data.
